## 5. Report for the coordinator

1. **`PF-R2`'s condition cannot be met, and two of its questions are refuted.** `Q1`–`Q3` are not observable on
   testnet (no partial fill at ≤5,000 USD). `Q4` (behaviour) and `Q6` (the chase) are refuted by the `11050` edit
   refusal. `docs/spec-partial-fill-fix.md` §0 says the coordinator then falls back to `PF-R2` option (b) —
   app-side resizing, `first_hit` kept. The coordinator applies that rule; this record supplies the evidence.
2. **`incremental` would break the order and stop paths the app relies on**, not only the partial-fill case: the
   entry chase, the bracket re-anchor, `Edit T.P.`, `Edit T.S.`, `B.E.`, and the triggered-SL chase (inferred from `TN-F1`; the entry chase, `Edit T.P.`, `Edit T.S.` and the triggered-SL
   chase were run, the re-anchor and `B.E.` were not). The M.SL emergency still works (it cancels and sends a market reduce; neither is an edit).
3. **The Phase 2 acceptance run as written cannot run on testnet.** `docs/spec-partial-fill-fix.md` §5 item 3 needs
   `X2_TAKER_ENTRY` to make a partial fill. Testnet's touch is ≥719,830 USD, so it never will at ≤5,000 USD. This is a
   spec defect for the owner before the Phase 2 seat starts (`TN-F2`).
4. **The emergency reduce-before-cancel question has one observation now** (open since X-1): a reduce-only market
   **fills in full** with a **triggered** reduce-only SL limit resting, and Deribit cancels that limit in the same
   message (A.12). Seen once, under `incremental`, not under `first_hit` (`TN-F6`).
5. **Batch 2, the `SB3` pin and its restart re-pin all behave as specified** (section 2 of this record). The pinned
   M.SL fired at the anchor minus 30, as the owner ruled (A.3).
