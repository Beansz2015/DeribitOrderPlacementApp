## 5. Report for the coordinator's batch-2 spec

1. **Dead stop-leg alert — the hook exists.** Branch on `order_state` **starting with `rejected`** for label
   `StopLossOrder` (`rejected:price_too_high` seen; the buy-side reason was not observed). Today it falls
   through every branch silently.
2. **"The exchange trails the SL" is false.** `trigger_offset` inside `otoco_config` is dropped. The comment
   at `frmMainPageV2.vb:3770` ("a native trailing stop … the exchange trails it") and any design that leans
   on it are wrong. The audit's trailing-trigger-offset row should be re-scoped from this fact.
3. **A reduce-only TP is not a safe fix for partial fills.** It makes every SL trigger cancel both legs and
   leaves the position naked; only the M.SL cap saved it here. The partial-fill fix needs another design.
4. **The partial-fill question cannot be answered on testnet** with the UI. Testnet filled 2000 USD at the
   top in one trade. Decide whether to design without the observation or to observe it live at 10 USD.
5. **A triggered stop is a new order id**, linked by `trigger_order_id`. Any id-tracking (dead-leg alert,
   no-stop watchdog, restore) must follow that link, not the `SLTS-…` id.
6. **The exchange reprices a crossing post-only stop limit** to the top of book at trigger. The chase does not
   need to rescue a crossed limit; its job is only to follow the market after that.
7. **Emergency close order:** a reduce-only market **does** fill while an **untriggered** reduce-only SL
   rests. The triggered-SL case is still open (section 4 of this document). Do not build reduce-before-cancel
   until it is observed.
8. **The live no-stop watchdog is justified twice over:** experiment (a) left a position with only a TP, and
   experiment (f) left one with nothing. In both the app logged nothing about the stop.
9. **Small item:** a `Mkt. BUY` fill prints no `Position entered` line (no `EntryMarketOrder` case).
